# Twisted Maze

## About the game

Twisted Maze is a physics puzzler with a twist — literally. Each level is a
maze floating in space, and instead of moving the ball, you spin the whole
maze. Drag to rotate the labyrinth and let gravity do the rest: guide the
ball through corridors, ramps and traps to reach the goal.

Collect the stars scattered along the way, unlock the next stage and spend
your progress on new ball skins. Levels get trickier as you go, mixing
sharper turns, moving hazards and tighter passages that demand precise
rotation timing.

## Controls

### Smartphone & tablet

- Drag anywhere on screen to rotate the maze.
- Tap the on-screen buttons to switch skins or go back home.

### Desktop

- Click and drag to rotate the maze.
- The ball rolls with gravity — plan your turns before you spin.
